# EU AI Act / AI Transparency Compliance

**Project:** GBB Software Update  
**Baseline date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** legacy / maintenance baseline

> Engineering/compliance record. Not a substitute for individual legal advice.

No direct user-facing AI interaction has been established by the current baseline. AI-assisted development alone does not require a blanket visible “AI-generated” label.

Before any new public release, verify whether the project introduces:

- direct AI chat, agent or avatar interaction;
- generative runtime text/audio/image/video output;
- synthetic content resembling real persons, objects, places, entities or events;
- emotion recognition or biometric categorisation;
- automated public-interest publishing without human editorial review;
- transfer of personal data to an external AI provider.

If direct AI interaction is added, users must be informed clearly from the start of the interaction where Article 50 applies. If synthetic media meets the legal deepfake criteria, apply the appropriate disclosure at first exposure. If the project becomes a provider of a generative AI system, assess machine-readable output marking under Article 50(2).

## Release gate

- [ ] Direct AI interaction assessed
- [ ] Generative outputs assessed
- [ ] Deepfake criteria assessed
- [ ] Human editorial review documented where relevant
- [ ] Privacy notice updated for external AI processing
- [ ] User-facing disclosure added where required
- [ ] Machine-readable marking assessed where provider duties apply

Legal baseline: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 50; Commission transparency guidelines published 20 July 2026. Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026.
